# Data safety worksheet — local camera with Google Play purchases

Audit date: 2026-10-01. Re-audit if dependencies, manifest permissions or functionality change. This is a preparation worksheet; complete and verify the actual Play Console form against the uploaded artifact.

| Data | Access and purpose | Retention / deletion | Transmission |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Live camera frames | Foreground viewfinder and user-triggered still capture | Session memory released on close | None |
| Saved JPEG photographs | User-created pictures in Pictures/PhyToy | User deletes through the system gallery; survive uninstall | None initiated by this app |
| Capture metadata | Date/time, exposure, ISO, focal length where available, lens direction and style | Stored inside the JPEG; deleted with photo | None |
| Default/last style, quality, grid, sound, haptics, review and permission state | Local app preferences | Clear app storage or uninstall | None |
| Photo URI/name/size/style/date index | Local photo list and review of pictures created by this app | Clear app storage or uninstall; original JPEGs remain | None |
| Runtime diagnostics | Local Android/device logs for failures and performance | Managed by Android; never automatically uploaded | None |
| Signed purchase receipts, product/token and entitlement state | Verify and cache four optional camera unlocks | Private local storage; clear app storage or uninstall removes the cache; Play purchases can be restored | Product queries, purchase and acknowledgement requests are handled by Google Play Billing |
| Payment method and Google Play account | Payment and restore via Google Play | Managed by Google under its own terms; app does not receive card details | Google Play payment service |

The build includes **Google Play Billing** and its billing/network permissions. It has no advertising ID, microphone or location permission. CAMERA is requested at runtime for expected foreground capture. Android 10+ uses MediaStore for its own photographs without READ_MEDIA_IMAGES or a broad library permission prompt. WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE is restricted to Android 9/API 28 and earlier; Android can imply legacy READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE from this permission. The app limits its photo list to its own PhyToy album and does not browse unrelated pictures. There is no publisher-added ad/analytics/crash-upload service or purchase server in this candidate. Billing 9.1.0 resolves Google Play services and Datatransport/CCT dependencies; their service diagnostics cannot be assumed absent. The merged manifest adds ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and a signature-level dynamic receiver permission, with no AD_ID, location or microphone permission. Backup is disabled for private app data.

Photos, frames, preferences and local purchase receipts are not sent to the publisher. Data processed only on the device is outside collection disclosure. Google documents a payment-service exception for financial details collected directly by that service and never accessed by the app, but purchase history and any other information received from a payment service require separate assessment. Re-audit the actual Billing SDK, uploaded artifact and Console form; do not copy the previous offline-only declaration unchanged. A future purchase-verification server or RTDN endpoint requires another policy/data-safety update before activation. [Official Data safety definitions](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469?hl=en), [payment-service disclosure rules](https://developer.android.com/about/versions/14/features/app-metadata#questions-about-completing-the-data-safety-section).

The app contains offline privacy text. The publisher must supply a privacy contact and make the same policy available at a stable public URL accessible without login or geographic restrictions. Use the prepared policy source, complete its publisher/contact fields, and verify the URL before submitting. [Official User Data policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311?hl=en).

Audit references for the added SDK: [Billing SDK Index](https://play.google.com/sdks/details/com-android-billingclient-billing), [financial-data declaration guidance](https://developer.android.com/privacy-and-security/declare-data-use#financial-info), [Google core SDK disclosure](https://developers.google.com/android/guides/play-data-disclosure). Core SDK statements cover only their named packages, not the complete Billing/CCT dependency graph. No complete public Billing 9.1.0/CCT data matrix was established during this audit; publisher review remains required before form submission.
