# Data safety worksheet — Play and Galaxy candidates

Audit date: 2026-10-06. Candidate: 0.17.0 / 18. This describes source behavior, not a completed store declaration or successful payment test. Reconcile it with the final signed APK/AAB, resolved dependencies, the published privacy policy and each store's current form before submitting.

| Channel | Package | Payment integration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Play | `com.ycolor.team.phytoy.camera.android.gpapp` | Google Play Billing 9.1.0; Play public-key signature verification |
| Galaxy Store | `com.ycolor.team.phytoy.camera.android.galaxyapp` | Samsung IAP 6.5.2; production receipt query and permanent Item acknowledgement |

Each variant includes its own billing SDK and permission. Purchases and restore belong to that store/package and the original store account; there is no publisher account or cross-store entitlement-transfer service. Empty billing configuration leaves paid cameras locked and prevents checkout; two cameras remain free. This does not establish that a configured SDK transmits no information.

The common manifest requests CAMERA, INTERNET and legacy WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE limited to API 28 and earlier; Android may imply legacy read permission. The photo code uses MediaStore and does not request READ_MEDIA_IMAGES or browse unrelated photo albums. It has no app-added location, microphone, advertising ID, analytics or crash-upload integration. Private app backup is disabled. Inspect the **merged manifest and dependency graph of each final variant** for SDK-added permissions and SDK behavior; absence of a permission does not prove absence of all data collection.

For Play, assess collection across the entire shipped SDK graph, not only publisher servers. On-device processing has a collection exception; a transfer to another app can count as sharing, with a specific user-initiated action exception that may apply to the explicit photo chooser. The payment-service exception does not automatically exempt purchase history received by the app. These are assessment rules, not preselected answers. [Official Data safety definitions](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469?hl=en), [financial-data guidance](https://developer.android.com/privacy-and-security/declare-data-use#financial-info).

For Galaxy, the receipt request is an actual off-device transaction-data flow even though the publisher runs no backend. The app verifies only documented production/success Item receipts for its own package/item/payment, rejects consumed/test receipts and grants only after acknowledgement and a durable local cache write. Samsung documents the [public receipt endpoint and response fields](https://developer.samsung.com/iap/programming-guide/samsung-iap-server-api.html). This client verification is not a deployed trusted publisher backend. Review Samsung's SDK/service disclosures and fill the Seller Portal privacy/Data Safety information for the Galaxy artifact. [Samsung registration guidance](https://developer.samsung.com/galaxy-store/launch.html), [distribution requirements](https://developer.samsung.com/galaxy-store/distribution-guide.html?lang=en).

**Unresolved before store submission:** no complete public data-type/retention matrix has been established here for Billing 9.1.0 and its transitive components, or Samsung IAP 6.5.2 and Samsung payment services. Do not submit a blanket “no collection/sharing” declaration. Obtain and reconcile provider disclosures, including identifiers, transaction history, diagnostics, transport and deletion/retention. Reference [Billing SDK Index](https://play.google.com/sdks/details/com-android-billingclient-billing), [Google core SDK disclosure](https://developers.google.com/android/guides/play-data-disclosure), [Samsung IAP release notes](https://developer.samsung.com/iap/release-note.html) and the [Samsung IAP license](https://developer.samsung.com/iap/terms/license-agreement.txt); a license or release note is not a complete privacy matrix.

Provide a real publisher name, support email and stable public HTTPS privacy URL through the release inputs; `phytoyRequirePublisherConfigured=true` validates those fields but cannot confirm the URL content/availability or the final forms. App deletion removes only local data; it does not request deletion of Google/Samsung purchase records, third-party shared copies or support email. Complete the policy's contact, retention and deletion process, verify it without login, and align the offline policy and both listings. [Google User Data policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311?hl=en).
