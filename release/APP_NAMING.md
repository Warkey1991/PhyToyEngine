# App naming decision

User-selected name, confirmed on 2026-10-02: **ToviCam: Retro Digital Camera**.

| Surface | Name | Characters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand and launcher label | ToviCam | 7 |
| Google Play title, English | ToviCam: Retro Digital Camera | 29 |
| Google Play title, Simplified Chinese | ToviCam - 复古数码相机 | 16 |

The proposed brand story draws on Toy, Vintage and Camera. This is a naming concept rather than a dictionary definition. The store title explains the retro digital-camera category; the in-app camera heading continues to show only the selected camera type.

[Google Play's metadata policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9898842?hl=en) limits titles to 30 characters and requires accurate, descriptive metadata. Naming is based on product fit, not measured search volume or a guaranteed ranking.

Public indexed searches on 2026-10-02 did not surface a clear matching ToviCam app. This is a limited public search, not confirmation of trademark availability or an exhaustive app-store search.

App display strings, new-photo software metadata and store artwork use ToviCam. The PhyToy engine name, technical identifiers and Pictures/PhyToy album remain for compatibility with existing photos and app data.

The localized titles and descriptions are maintained in [google-play-listing.md](google-play-listing.md).
