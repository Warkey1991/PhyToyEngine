Camera and image processing

PhyToy Camera uses camera access while the camera screen is active to provide a live viewfinder and take photographs at your request. Images are processed on your device. Temporary frame data is released when the camera session closes. The app does not request microphone or location access.

Photos and metadata

Photos are saved in your device's Pictures/PhyToy album. JPEG metadata may include capture time, exposure, ISO, focal length, lens direction and the selected style. The app does not add location metadata. You can delete photographs using your system gallery. Uninstalling the app does not delete photographs saved in shared device storage.

Local settings

The app stores camera preferences (default/last style, photo quality, grid, sound, haptics and review), permission-request state and a local index of photographs you created with the app in private local storage. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app removes these settings. Private app data is excluded from Android backup.

Collection and sharing

This build has no Internet permission, account system, advertising, analytics or server-upload service. We do not receive or share your photographs, camera frames or settings. Device logs may contain local diagnostic information; the app does not upload those logs. If you choose to send a support request, the information you include is handled by the support channel you select and its provider.

Your controls

You can revoke camera permission in Android Settings → Apps → PhyToy Camera → Permissions. On Android 9 and earlier, storage permission is used to save and review your own photos; newer versions use Android MediaStore without broad library access. Use the system gallery to delete photos, and Android app settings to clear local app data.

Changes and contact

If data handling changes, this policy and the in-app explanation will be updated. Questions about this policy can be directed to the privacy contact above.
