Camera access is used for the foreground viewfinder and photos you choose to take. Images are processed on your device. Temporary frames are released when the session closes. The app does not request location or microphone access.

Photos are stored in Pictures/PhyToy. JPEG metadata includes capture time, exposure and camera style, without added location data. Delete photos in the app or your system gallery; uninstalling does not remove saved photographs. Sharing sends only the photo you select to the destination app you choose.

Camera preferences, permission state, the photo index and protected purchase receipts are stored privately on your device. Clearing app storage removes this local data. Purchases can be restored through the original store using the same Google Play or Samsung account. Purchases from different stores do not automatically transfer.

Purchases and restoration use Google Play or Galaxy Store and require a network connection. The store processes payments under its own terms and privacy policy; ToviCam does not receive bank card details. In the Galaxy Store edition, purchase verification also sends the purchase identifier to Samsung. The app has no independent account, advertisements, photo uploads or publisher analytics service. Payment SDKs may process their own service diagnostics. Local diagnostics are not automatically uploaded by the app.

Camera permissions can be revoked in Android Settings → Apps → ToviCam → Permissions. Support messages are sent only when you choose to contact us, through your email app.

ToviCam 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 6 日

相机权限用于前台取景和你主动拍摄的照片。图像在本机处理，会话关闭后释放临时画面。应用不请求定位或麦克风权限。

照片保存在 Pictures/PhyToy。JPEG 元数据包含拍摄时间、曝光和相机风格，不添加定位信息。可在应用内或系统相册删除照片；卸载应用不会删除已保存的照片。分享仅将你选择的照片交给你选定的应用。

相机偏好、权限状态、照片索引和受保护的购买凭证保存在本机私有存储。清除应用数据可删除这些本地数据。购买可在原购买商店通过同一Google Play／Samsung 账号恢复，不同商店的购买不会自动互通。

购买和恢复通过 Google Play 或 Galaxy Store 进行，需要联网。商店按其条款和隐私政策处理付款，ToviCam 不会收到银行卡信息。Galaxy Store 版本还会向三星发送购买标识以核验购买。应用没有独立账号、广告、照片上传或发布者统计分析服务；支付 SDK 可能处理自身服务诊断数据。应用不会自动上传本地诊断日志。

可在 Android 设置 → 应用 → ToviCam → 权限中撤销相机权限。仅当你主动联系支持时，信息才会通过你选择的邮件应用发送。
