Full description (EN):

ToviCam brings six expressive camera styles to everyday photography. Discover vivid daylight color, deep monochrome, warm digital character, square plastic-camera softness, punchy street color and fisheye distortion.

See the look as you shoot. Tap to focus, adjust exposure and zoom with sliders or gestures, and switch between front and back cameras. Choose a flash mode when your camera supports it. Browse your shots, zoom in, move between photos, share a selected picture through Android's share chooser or confirm its deletion. Choose photo quality, a composition grid, capture feedback and optional immediate review in camera settings. JPEG photos are saved to Pictures/PhyToy with capture and style metadata.

Image processing happens on your device. No separate app account, advertising, custom analytics or automatic photo uploads. If you choose to share, the app you select receives access to that picture and may handle it under its own terms. DH 2++ color and monochrome are free; the other four cameras are sold separately as one-time purchases. Try their live previews before unlocking photography. Purchases and restoration require Google Play and a network connection; confirmed unlocks are cached for offline shooting. The app does not request location access. Photos remain in your system gallery after you uninstall the app unless you delete them separately.

Preview speed adapts to device performance and temperature. Output resolution depends on the style and the camera capabilities. Preview and saved photos use the same imaging pipeline; fine detail and random noise may differ with resolution and the phone camera's processing.

Requires a supported 64-bit Android device with Vulkan 1.1 and camera hardware-buffer support. Camera styles are independent interpretations, not certified reproductions or manufacturer-endorsed products.
