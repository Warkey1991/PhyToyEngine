# Google Play listing draft

App name: **PhyToy Camera** (13 characters).

Short description (EN): **Six expressive camera styles. Shoot and develop photos entirely on your device.**

Short description (zh-CN): **六种玩具相机风格，实时取景、拍摄与回看，在本机完成成像。**

Full description (EN):

PhyToy Camera brings six expressive camera styles to everyday photography. Discover vivid daylight color, deep monochrome, warm digital character, square plastic-camera softness, punchy street color and fisheye distortion.

See the look as you shoot. Tap to focus, slide to adjust exposure and pinch to zoom. Switch between front and back cameras, choose a flash mode when your camera supports it, and browse your shots in the photo list. Choose photo quality, a composition grid, capture feedback and optional immediate review in camera settings. JPEG photos are saved to Pictures/PhyToy with capture and style metadata.

Image processing happens on your device. No account, advertising, analytics or photo uploads. The app does not request location access. Photos remain in your system gallery after you uninstall the app.

Preview speed adapts to device performance and temperature. Output resolution depends on the style and the camera capabilities. Preview and saved photos use the same imaging pipeline; fine detail and random noise may differ with resolution and the phone camera's processing.

Requires a supported 64-bit Android device with Vulkan 1.1 and camera hardware-buffer support. Camera styles are independent interpretations, not certified reproductions or manufacturer-endorsed products.

Full description (zh-CN):

PhyToy Camera 为日常拍摄带来六种相机风格：鲜明的日光色彩、深邃黑白、温暖数码、方形塑料相机、浓郁街头色彩和鱼眼畸变。

实时查看成像效果，轻点对焦，上下滑动调节曝光，双指缩放。支持前后镜头切换，在硬件支持时选择闪光模式；在照片列表中查看拍过的照片。设置页可调整照片质量、构图网格、拍照反馈和拍后回看。JPEG 照片保存在 Pictures/PhyToy，包含拍摄参数与风格元数据。

图像处理在手机上完成，没有账号、广告、统计分析或照片上传，不请求定位权限。卸载应用不会删除系统相册中的照片。

预览帧率根据手机性能和温度自动调整，成片分辨率取决于风格和相机能力。预览与成片使用同一成像流程，细节和随机噪声可能因分辨率与手机相机处理而略有差异。
