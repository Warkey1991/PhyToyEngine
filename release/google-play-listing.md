Full description (EN):

Turn everyday moments into photos with character. ToviCam brings the mood of retro digital and toy cameras to your phone, with six distinct styles and a live preview that lets you see the look before you shoot.

Find your camera style
- DH 2++: vivid daylight color, rich primaries and soft detail.
- DH 2++ B&W: deep blacks, bright highlights and a soft monochrome look.
- DIGITAL 01: warm highlights, cool shadows and subtle lens character.
- PLASTIC 82: square photos with soft edges and plastic-camera character.
- STREET 84: punchy street color, a 2:3 frame and dark corners.
- FISHEYE 05: square photos with barrel distortion and circular light falloff.

Simple controls, more room to explore
Tap to focus, slide to adjust exposure and pinch to zoom. Switch between front and back cameras, choose a supported flash mode and use a composition grid to frame your shot. Adjust photo quality, capture feedback and optional photo review in settings.

Keep, browse and share
Save JPEG photos to your device. Browse your shots, zoom in on details, move between photos and share a selected picture through your preferred app. Photos stay in your system gallery after uninstalling ToviCam unless you delete them separately.

Start with two free cameras
DH 2++ color and monochrome are free to use. Try the live previews of the other four camera styles before buying a permanent photo-capture unlock.

Choose an individual camera or the All Cameras Lifetime Pass, which includes all current and future camera styles added to ToviCam. All purchases are one-time payments. No subscription is required. Previous individual camera purchases are not deducted from the pass price. Google Play displays your local currency and final price at checkout.

Purchasing and restoring require Google Play and an internet connection. Restore purchases in the Google Play version using the same Google Play account. Confirmed unlocks are stored on your device for offline shooting. Purchases from other stores do not automatically transfer.

Processed on your phone
Image processing happens on your device, with no automatic photo uploads. No separate app account or ads. ToviCam does not request location access. Sharing sends your selected photo to the app you choose.

Requires a compatible 64-bit device running Android 8.0 or later with Vulkan 1.1 and camera hardware-buffer support. Camera features and photo resolution vary by device and style. Preview and saved photos may differ slightly in fine detail and noise. Camera styles are independent interpretations and are not endorsed by camera manufacturers.
