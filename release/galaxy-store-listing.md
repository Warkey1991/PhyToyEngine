Full description (EN):

ToviCam brings six expressive camera styles to everyday photography. Discover vivid daylight color, deep monochrome, warm digital character, square plastic-camera softness, punchy street color and fisheye distortion.

See the look as you shoot. Tap to focus, slide to adjust exposure and pinch to zoom. Switch between front and back cameras and choose a flash mode when the camera supports it. Adjust photo quality, a composition grid, capture feedback and optional immediate review in settings.

Browse your photos, move between shots, share a selected picture through Android's share chooser or confirm its deletion. JPEG photos are saved to Pictures/PhyToy with capture and style metadata. Image processing happens on your device; the app does not automatically upload your photos. If you choose to share, the app you select receives access to that picture and may handle it under its own terms. Photos remain in the system gallery after uninstalling ToviCam unless you delete them separately.

DH 2++ color and monochrome are free. The other four cameras are sold separately as one-time unlocks. Try their live previews before unlocking photography. The local currency and final price are shown by Galaxy Store at checkout.

Purchasing and restoring require Galaxy Store, a Samsung account and a network connection. After reinstalling or changing devices, restore through Galaxy Store with the Samsung account used for the original purchase. Confirmed unlocks are cached for offline shooting. Google Play purchases do not automatically unlock this Galaxy Store version, and Galaxy Store purchases do not automatically transfer to Google Play. ToviCam has no separate app account.

The app has no advertising or publisher-added analytics service and does not request location access. Galaxy Store and Samsung IAP handle payment-service information; the app checks Samsung receipts to confirm camera unlocks. See the privacy policy for details.

Preview speed adapts to device performance and temperature. Output resolution depends on the style and camera capabilities. Preview and saved photos use the same imaging pipeline; fine detail and random noise may differ with resolution and the phone camera's processing.

Requires Android 8.0 or later on a compatible 64-bit device with Vulkan 1.1 and camera hardware-buffer support. Camera styles are independent interpretations, not certified reproductions or manufacturer-endorsed products.

需要 Android 8.0 或以上、支持 Vulkan 1.1 和相机硬件缓冲的兼容 64 位设备。所有风格均为独立诠释，不是经过认证的真机复刻，也未获得相机厂商背书。
